# Omnichannel Promotion Skill

This skill provides expertise in promoting anything across modern search engines, AI models, and traditional digital marketing channels. It bridges the gap between traditional search optimization and the new era of AI-driven discovery.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**:
   - Keyword research, on-page optimization, and technical SEO recommendations.
   - Content structure strategies to rank higher on Google, Bing, and other traditional search engines.
   - Backlink strategy, meta tag optimization, and local SEO.

2. **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)**:
   - Formatting content to be easily digestible by Answer Engines (like Google's AI Overviews or voice assistants).
   - Structuring data using Schema.org (JSON-LD) for rich snippets, featured snippets, and knowledge graphs.
   - Q&A style content optimization, FAQ structuring, and entity-based relationship mapping.

3. **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**:
   - Strategies to ensure brands, products, and services are recommended by LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity).
   - Ensuring the brand or product is easily retrieved via RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) by creating authoritative, citation-worthy content.
   - Brand positioning for AI model training data, including press mentions, high-authority domain publishing, and structured knowledge bases.

4. **Omnichannel & Viral Promotion**:
   - Social media growth strategies (Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, etc.).
   - Community building, email marketing, and engagement tactics.
   - Content repurposing and cross-pollination across different mediums (text, video, audio).

## Usage Guidelines

- Always clarify the target audience, the product/service being promoted, and the primary goal before designing a promotion strategy.
- When generating content, provide clear HTML/markdown structures that are optimized for both human readers and AI scrapers.
- Explicitly differentiate strategies based on the channel: what works for traditional Google Search (SEO) might differ from what works for Perplexity (GEO).
- Provide actionable, step-by-step checklists for the user when launching a new promotion campaign, ensuring they cover SEO, AEO, and GEO bases.
