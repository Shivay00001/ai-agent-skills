# Marketing and PR Skill

This skill provides expertise in developing and executing marketing campaigns, public relations strategies, and brand communications.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Content Strategy & Creation**:
   - Drafting engaging blog posts, newsletters, and social media content.
   - Planning content calendars and campaign timelines.

2. **Public Relations (PR)**:
   - Writing professional press releases for product launches or company announcements.
   - Developing outreach strategies for journalists and influencers.
   - Creating media kits and press materials.

3. **SEO & Growth Marketing**:
   - Identifying keyword opportunities and optimizing content for search engines.
   - Crafting conversion-focused landing page copy.
   - Strategies for community growth and user acquisition.

4. **Brand Management**:
   - Ensuring consistent tone of voice across all communications.
   - Developing brand guidelines and messaging frameworks.

## Usage Guidelines

- Tailor the tone of voice to the specific platform (e.g., professional for LinkedIn, casual for Twitter/X).
- When drafting press releases, follow the standard format: FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE, Headline, Dateline, Introduction, Body, Quote, Call to Action, About the Company, Media Contact.
- Always consider the target audience and the primary goal (e.g., brand awareness, lead generation) when proposing marketing strategies.
